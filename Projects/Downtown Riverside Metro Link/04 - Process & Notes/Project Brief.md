## Creative Direction
A station-wide holiday program built around the "Holiday Express." idea — branded pole
banners read by day, warm-white evening lighting turns the platforms and plaza into a
destination after sundown, and a vocabulary of wreaths, garlands, and feature elements
gives every part of the station a holiday role. The Mission Inn–style "City of Riverside"
Bell Display anchors the Signature tier as a civic-pride centerpiece.

## Customer Goals
- Position the Downtown Riverside MetroLink station as a regional holiday destination after dark
- Drive non-transit foot traffic and dwell time at the station plaza during the program window
- Reinforce RCTC's visible investment in the Downtown Riverside corridor

## Customer Constraints
- All decor must clear platform-edge dynamic envelope and station signage sightlines
- Install and removal outside revenue service hours
- Materials must withstand winter Santa Ana wind events

## Success Criteria
- Measurable increase in evening visitors to the station plaza during the program window
- Local press and social media coverage of the activation
- Zero MetroLink operational disruptions during install and strike

## What You're Approving
The 2026 Downtown Riverside MetroLink Holiday Program — station-wide decor, lighting,
and feature installations live November 20, 2026 through January 5, 2027, at the tier
and à-la-carte add-ons you select on the Investment page.
