## Who we are

St. Nicks has been in business since **1998** — 29 years of commercial holiday and seasonal lighting. Two distinct brands operate under the company:

### Brand 1: St. Nicks Service Installation
- **Website:** st-nicks.com
- **What we do:** Commercial holiday and seasonal lighting + custom decor — design, install, maintenance, takedown, and storage
- **Customers:** **100% commercial.** Active customer segments:
  - Municipalities
  - Public plazas / downtown districts
  - Shopping centers
  - Mixed-use / lifestyle centers
  - Airports
  - Amusement parks
  - Casinos
  - Resorts
  - Hotels
  - Performing arts venues (e.g., LA Music Center)
  - Convention centers
  - Stadiums / arenas / sports venues
  - Universities / college campuses
  - Office parks / corporate campuses
- **We do NOT work with:** residential properties, **HOAs (homeowner associations)**, hospitals / medical campuses
- **Geographic service area:**
  - **Self-perform:** Southern California, within ~4 hours of HQ
  - **National capability:** through a network of install partners — we have done installs across the country, but most volume is currently in CA. **Building out the national footprint is an active growth area.**
- **Average project size:** $50K – $150K
- **Differentiators:**
  - **28-year track record** — major commercial portfolio, deep experience
  - **Commercial-grade quality** of materials and install execution
  - **Full-service / turnkey** — design, install, maintenance, takedown, storage. Customer never touches it.
  - **Custom decor capability** — we design and build custom display pieces, not just hang strands

### Brand 2: St. Nicks Christmas Lighting (E-commerce)
- **Website:** stnickschristmaslighting.com
- **What we do:** Online retail of commercial-grade Christmas lighting and seasonal decor
- **Customers:** Mix of homeowners, professional installers, and **municipal buyers**
- **Average order value:** $200 – $1,000
- **Product mix:**
  - C9 / C7 bulbs and strands
  - Mini lights
  - Garland and wreaths
  - Large artificial trees (pre-lit and pre-decorated)
  - Walkthrough displays, banners, and other seasonal displays
  - Custom decor and accessories
- **Differentiators:**
  - **Commercial-grade quality at retail access** — same product spec the pros install, available to anyone
  - **Expert support from real installers** — we do this for a living, not a side hustle
  - **Custom and hard-to-find decor** — large displays, walkthroughs, banners, items you can't get on Amazon

## We manufacture our own product

A core differentiator across both brands: **St. Nicks manufactures product in China and Mexico.** This is unusual in the industry — most installers and lighting retailers resell. We control the supply chain factory to property, which gives us:
- Quality control
- Margin advantage
- Custom product capability
- Supply continuity through seasonal demand spikes

When positioning the company in proposals, sales conversations, or customer service — this is a fact worth surfacing.

## How we operate across the year

We are **year-round** — not a seasonal business that goes dark in spring.

| Period | What's happening |
|--------|------------------|
| **Spring (Jan–May)** | Heavy tradeshow season. Tear-down of Christmas installs in January. Other seasonal installations (non-Christmas) running Jan–Oct. |
| **Q2 end / Q3 / early Q4** | Most contracts get signed. Quoting and proposal activity is at its peak. |
| **September – early December** | Christmas install execution. |
| **January** | Christmas tear-down + storage. |

Implications:
- "Off-season" doesn't really exist — there's always a contract being negotiated, a tradeshow being prepped, or a non-Christmas seasonal install being executed
- Sales pressure is highest end-Q2 through early Q4 (contract signing window)
- Operations capacity is most constrained Sept – early Dec
