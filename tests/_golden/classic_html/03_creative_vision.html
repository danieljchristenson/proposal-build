Creative Vision

The design direction for the 2026 MetroLink program.
Design Direction
Holiday Express.

A civic-scale holiday aesthetic that turns the MetroLink line itself into the holiday gesture. Wreaths and garlands frame each station entrance like a ceremonial gateway; evening lighting turns the platforms themselves into destinations after sundown. The same design vocabulary repeats at every stop so the line reads as one program from end to end.

WELCOME

Wreaths and garlands at every station entrance — the holiday begins at the curb.

JOURNEY

Pole banners and platform lighting carry the design language down the line.

ARRIVAL

Walk-through ornament and lit displays at end-of-line — a destination, not a transfer.
