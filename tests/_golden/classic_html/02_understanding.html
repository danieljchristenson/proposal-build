Our Understanding

Playback of discovery — so we're all working from the same page.
VENUE & CONTEXT
The MetroLink line connects six communities across Riverside County. Holiday season foot-traffic spikes at flagship Downtown Riverside; outer stations carry mostly commuter and park-and-ride traffic with civic-pride significance for local residents.
GOALS FOR 2026
Establish RCTC's MetroLink line as a regional holiday destination; drive non-transit foot traffic to Downtown Riverside in particular; position the County as a leader in civic seasonal programming.
KEY CONSTRAINTS
All decor must clear MetroLink overhead catenary safety envelope. Install and removal must occur outside revenue service hours. Materials must withstand winter Santa Ana wind events.
WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
Measurable increase in evening visitors during the program window. Local press and social media coverage of the activation. Zero MetroLink operational disruptions during install/strike.
